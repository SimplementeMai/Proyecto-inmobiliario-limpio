# Specification: Schedule Visit Screen

## Overview
This track implements the 'Schedule Visit' feature, allowing potential buyers to book appointments to view properties.

## Requirements
- Booking form with date and time selection.
- Integration with property ID.
- Alignment with `prd/schedule_visit_screen/screen.png`.

## Components
- `VisitBookingForm`: Main booking form.
- `CalendarPicker`: Date/time selection component.
